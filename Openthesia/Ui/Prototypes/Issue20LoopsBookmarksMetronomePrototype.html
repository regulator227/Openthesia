Practice navigation and timing controls

This logic demo lets a Learner create loops and bookmarks, move around a Chart, and feel how count-in and metronome choices interact with a Practice Session.

Question under test: How should loops, bookmarks, count-in, and metronome controls behave across Practice Modes, Required Hands, tempo changes, seeking, loop repeats, and restarts after errors?

Working decisions to challenge

  • Loops and bookmarks are personal Chart navigation, never Practice Modes.
  • An enabled loop defines the fixed Practice Range; every pass is a new Practice Session.
  • A bookmark outside the loop disables the loop and makes the new attempt assisted.
  • Count-in freezes Chart time before starts, restarts, and discontinuous resumes.
  • Count-in clicks are independent of the during-practice metronome toggle.
  • Mode, Required Hands, tempo, and range changes restart the comparable attempt; metronome and future count-in settings do not.

1. Practice setup

These controls stay separate. Mode, Required Hands, and tempo change the comparable setup; metronome and count-in tune guidance.

2. Transport and Learner input

Advance one beat at a time; the state shows the tempo-dependent click cadence. Wait for Notes advances only when the required notes are supplied, and errors never restart automatically.

3. Loops

Mark boundaries at the playhead, or use “End after playhead” to include the current/final beat. Save a named loop, then enable it; editing an enabled loop starts a new Practice Session at its new range.

4. Bookmarks

Bookmarks are named personal waypoints. Navigating to one is a seek; moving outside an enabled loop turns that loop off instead of silently clamping the destination.
